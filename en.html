Bassin Registre

Privacy policy and legal notice — version of 12 October 2026 · Français

In short: Bassin Registre runs entirely on your device. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no analytics. We receive none of your data.

What the app does

Bassin Registre keeps the water log of pools open to the public and the domestic hot-water (Legionella) monitoring register: time-stamped readings, regulatory thresholds shown with their source, alerts, technical log and PDF or CSV exports. It is a record-keeping tool: it is neither an approval nor a certificate and does not replace the operator's obligations.

Data stored

  • On your device only (local database): establishments, pools, readings (chlorine, pH, temperature, clarity…), attendance, meter readings, technical log, hot-water measuring points and readings, export history and fingerprints.
  • Operators' first names: only a first name is asked for. The optional 4-digit code is never stored in clear: only a salted SHA-256 hash is kept.
  • Photos you attach (laboratory report, network diagram): copied into the app's private storage on the device.
  • Preferences: language, current establishment and operator, reminder times.

No personal health data, no address and no advertising identifier is collected.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you photograph a document for the register.
  • Photos: only when you choose a picture to attach.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on the morning and afternoon reading reminders; these are local notifications scheduled on the device.

The app never asks for location, microphone or contacts.

What leaves the device, and only when you ask

  • Exports (pool log PDF, Legionella register PDF, CSV) and backups: created on the device, then handed to the system share sheet for the app you choose (Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email…). A backup can be encrypted with a passphrase only you know.
  • Links: opening a regulatory text or the app's store page opens your browser or the store.

Any review prompt is displayed by the system (App Store or Google Play); the app does not receive your rating.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the device until you delete it. Uninstalling the app erases its data; make a backup first. Regulations set retention periods for the register: the app reminds you to keep your backups.

Your rights

Because we hold none of your data, we cannot view, correct or delete it remotely: you keep full control on your device. Questions: sogerg.enterprise@gmail.com.
